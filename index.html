-- about me

From weather models to the numbers businesses run on

I studied meteorology at Institut Teknologi Bandung, where I spent most of my time cleaning, merging and modelling messy data: 20 years of satellite rain, river discharge from 12 WRF-Hydro runs, and CMIP6 climate projections.

Today I point the same habits at public and business data. I scrape what isn't published as a table, load it into SQL, check it twice, and turn it into dashboards and models that answer one clear question.

I hold myself to high standards and keep improving, one project at a time. Off the clock I play basketball, badminton and padel, play guitar and piano, and lose a few rounds of CS:GO, Valorant, FIFA or NBA 2K.
